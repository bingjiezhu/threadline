# From a compelling demo to a paying user

These are drafts and an operating plan. Nothing has been sent and no prospect list was harvested.

## First cohort

Independent technical consultants and owners of 2–10-person AI/software implementation firms. Qualify for weekly client conversations, several paused or active projects, existing permitted text notes, and a recent costly episode of forgotten context or follow-through. Exclude “I have lots of contacts” without an actual recurring task.

## Warm invitation draft

Subject: A small test for conversations worth remembering

Hi [Name],

When a client says “come back once you can do X,” how do you remember to reconnect after X becomes possible?

I’m building Threadline: a lightweight private workspace that connects a saved conversation, the person behind it, and the next step. It also lets you compare an old project requirement with a newly confirmed capability—without treating a match as a sale.

I’m looking for a few independent consultants to test it with a handful of their own permitted notes. We would observe whether it actually saves maintenance time and helps recover useful context. The proposed guided pilot is $49 per month, agreed separately; there is no payment in the app.

Would a 20-minute look at one recent example from your work be useful? No full inbox import is needed.

[Your real name]

## Interview, before the demo

Ask: “Tell me about the last time you needed to find someone you had already met.” Then ask what they searched, what was missing, how often it happened, what consequence followed, what workaround they use and whether they already pay for it. Ask permission before seeing any private material. Keep notes of non-painful examples as well as enthusiastic ones.

Show one capture and let the user choose a different real topic. Do not coach them into the intended answer. Ask what they would stop using or doing if this worked; a product with no displacement may simply add maintenance.

## Activation

Import no more than five existing notes for the first trial. The user chooses the person and what may be saved. Observe time to the first correct source retrieval and a next-step decision; record operator help separately. Avoid measuring speed only after repeated rehearsals.

## Scorecard

Track approached → qualified → offered → paid → activated → active week 4 → second payment. Every stage has its denominator. Record total customer maintenance minutes, operator minutes, coverage of important conversations, useful/not-useful retrieval and actual follow-through. App counters are self-recorded behavior, not verified sales.

## Publication draft

“The next opportunity might already be in a conversation you forgot.

Threadline lets you save the part that matters, find the person through your original words, and keep the promise you made. When project conditions change, it helps you compare then and now—without inventing budget, intent or trust.

This is a working self-hosted release with optional AI. The walkthrough uses fictional records on the real backend. I’m looking for independent consultants to test the workflow with a small amount of their own permitted context.”

Attach the current walkthrough and a real application/repository address only after it has actually been deployed/published. No fabricated testimonial, user count, logo, press quote or conversion rate.

## Where to test acquisition

Begin with direct professional introductions and communities where the founder already has permission to participate. Share a source-backed before/after example and the maintenance-time question, not repeated promotional blasts. Ask for genuine peer introductions after a user gets a useful result. Broader paid acquisition comes after renewal and support-cost evidence.
