# Warm Start: first customers and promotion

Read `STARTUP_THESIS.md` for the buyer/user distinction and stop conditions. All actions below are planned, not completed outreach.

## Interview invitation (English draft)

“Hi [Name], I am testing a small onboarding tool that connects a first task to its original source and the colleague who currently owns the next step. Could we walk through one recent case where a new hire had to reconstruct that context? A 20-minute conversation would help us understand the current process. No private company data is needed for the interview.”

Use only contacts you are entitled to approach; respect declines. Replace the name manually and do not mass-send this draft through chat adapters.

## Questions

Ask about the last actual first-week task, not enthusiasm about AI. Where did they look? Which source was stale? Who corrected it? What was the elapsed time versus active effort? Was a buddy already sufficient? What information could the organization permit an app to store? Who could authorize a paid pilot?

## Website story

Headline: **A new team. A clear next step.**

Subhead: **Find the source, the colleague and the path behind your first task.**

Show the old Maya route, the later Jordan source and the remaining unknowns. Label it fictional. End with an invitation to test a real first task, not a claim of an already-integrated enterprise platform.

The existing landing inquiry form stores explicit consent and contact details locally. It sends no email, charges nothing and is not a complete sales operation. The operator must handle responses and deletion requests.

## Four-week pilot outline

Week 1: qualify managers, get permission, establish the current workflow and collect a small source set. Week 2: observe actual capture and first plans, track corrections. Week 3: observe changed owners or sources and completed outcomes without constant founder prompting. Week 4: request a real continuation decision at the stated price, document reasons and support hours. Separate paid software, manual setup and free trials.

## Public claim rules

Say “signed Slack/Discord adapters implemented” until live installation passes. Say “optional AI” unless the configured model is actually running. Say “new-hire pilot hypothesis” until paid behavior exists. No sponsor logo implies endorsement. Do not present a test count as security certification. Never present the whole prior Threadline codebase as new during the hackathon.
