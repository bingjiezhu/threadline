Designed first for independent consultants and owners of small AI or software implementation firms. A personal workspace for the person who handles discovery, delivery, and the next client conversation. Shared team workspaces are not part of this release.

START WITH A SMALL, HONEST TEST

Prove it in your own work.

Use the self-hosted application with your own records. A proposed guided pilot can help a founder test whether the workflow saves maintenance time and creates useful next steps.
