YOUR FIRST WORKSPACE

A practical guide to Threadline.

A private workspace for consultants and service-company owners who handle their own client relationships. You can manage your real contacts, projects, original notes, and follow-ups. Optional fictional examples are available for learning, but no sample data is loaded by default.

1. Start the application.

Install Node.js 22.16 or newer (Node 24 LTS is the recommended deployment baseline). In the project folder, run npm start, then visit http://localhost:4173. No npm dependency installation or model key is needed for core management. Keep the server running while you use the app.

Create your account with a password of at least 12 characters. Choose your workspace name and timezone in Settings. By default, the first account can be created locally; further registrations close. An operator can configure an invitation code for additional accounts, each with its own separate workspace.

Start with your own records.

Add one contact, one live project, and one real conversation. Learning with examples is optional: download the fictional workspace archive and import it into an empty account through Settings. Do not restore an example over your real data.

2. Connect people to the work.

In Contacts, select New contact. Add a name, email, company, role, and tags. Names alone are not used to merge people. CSV import supports the provided template, quoted fields, and up to 1,000 rows. Duplicate emails are reported rather than silently overwritten.

In Projects, create the outcome you are working toward. Set a primary contact, goal, commercial stage, optional value and review date, and the things you do not yet know. The displayed value is the amount you enter—not expected revenue or an AI forecast.

You can edit, archive, or delete records. “Do not contact” blocks generated follow-ups for that person. Deleting a contact removes the contact record and its linked suggestions; original notes may still mention the person and must be reviewed separately.

3. Save the source. Review the meaning.

Use Add note to paste an authorized meeting note, email, or delivery update. You can import UTF-8 .txt or .md files. Other file formats and direct Gmail, LinkedIn, or WeChat synchronization are not included. Set when the conversation actually happened, or leave the date unknown. Save first.

In the note, select an exact passage and choose Add evidence. You may also use Find candidate sentences, a local text scanner, or optional AI extraction when configured. Neither method automatically approves evidence.

FieldWhat to enter
TypeRequirement, capability, blocker, commitment, context, contact change, or opinion.
TopicA shared label, such as private-deployment or single-sign-on. Local matching requires the same normalized topic.
CertaintyConfirmed in the source, uncertain, planned/conditional, or reported/hearsay. “Confirmed” is your interpretation of the source, not an independent audit.
Exact quotationA unique verbatim passage from this note. The app checks the characters and stores their location.
ExpiryAn optional date after which the evidence should not drive a new suggestion.

A confirmed capability needs an event date. A requirement needs a linked project. A plan such as “we expect to support this next quarter” must not be treated as a delivered capability. Review candidates and explicitly approve the interpretations you trust.

4. Look for changed conditions.

Open Follow-ups and select Refresh opportunities. This release matches your approved, confirmed project requirements against later, approved, confirmed capabilities on the same topic. It uses no fixed customer IDs and works with your newly created records.

An example: a client previously required private deployment; a newer delivery note confirms a relevant implementation. The result is a reason to recheck that project—not proof of present-day demand. A later confirmed blocker on the same topic suppresses the match. Planned, expired, or unapproved capabilities do not qualify.

Open a card to compare both quotations, the people involved, and the remaining unknowns. Accept update adds the context to a new project version without changing the project’s commercial stage or inventing a budget.

5. Draft carefully. Record reality.

Choose Prepare a draft and select only the quotations you permit in the outward-facing message. The local template asks a neutral question. Optional AI can draft from those selected quotations; it is never allowed to send. Edit the result and save it.

Copying a draft only records a copy. Send through your own email or messaging tool, then use Record contact with the actual time. Record a reply, meeting, or business outcome only after it happens. No response is inferred from silence. Use Activity to correct the latest mistaken business event; the original audit entry remains visible.

Dismiss an irrelevant match or snooze one until a future date. Snoozed items are reconsidered on refresh after that date. There are no background email or push reminders in this release.

6. Make it a weekly habit.

Create tasks directly from any workspace or project—no AI or matching evidence is required. Add an owner-facing title, due date, and notes. Mark tasks complete, reopen them, or cancel them.

After an important conversation, capture the source and review the few facts that matter. Once a week, check outstanding tasks, refresh opportunities, review current project ownership and timing, and record actual outcomes. Track how much extra maintenance this takes; do not build a second job around your relationship tool.

7. Take your data with you.

Settings includes a password-confirmed JSON export of your core records and business history. Store exports privately. Import into an empty workspace recreates contacts, projects, notes, evidence, and tasks with new IDs. Historical event and generated-draft snapshots remain in the archive; they are not replayed as new business activity. Run Refresh to recompute suggestions.

For an exact system recovery, the operator can use the full SQLite backup command described in docs/OPERATIONS.md. Full backups include account and session information and must be protected.

Deleting a note clears its body, quotations, and directly dependent generated context and drafts from the running application. An in-flight AI result cannot restore it. Existing external exports or backups are not remotely erased. Deleting your entire account removes the workspace and invalidates your sessions.

Understand AI and privacy.

Basic records, local scanning, exact-topic matching, templates, search, export, and deletion work without an API key. A server operator can enable OpenAI extraction and drafting with OPENAI_API_KEY. Only the explicitly selected note or drafting quotations are sent. The key is never exposed to the browser.

AI suggestions can be wrong. All extracted items require review. The app requests store: false; this is not a promise of zero provider retention. Consult the provider’s current data controls. No live external-model evaluation is included in the release’s test evidence.

Passwords are salted and hashed; sessions are server-side and HttpOnly. Records are scoped to the signed-in account. The SQLite database and exports are not end-to-end encrypted. Anyone with operator access to the server can access data. Use disk encryption, private backups, and HTTPS for remote access. The app is not certified for regulated data.

Common questions.

Why are there no suggestions?

Check that the project is active or waiting, the requirement and capability are both approved and confirmed, their topics match, the capability is dated later, neither has expired, and no later confirmed blocker or contact restriction applies. Zero matches is a valid result.

Why is a suggestion stale?

A source, approved interpretation, contact restriction, or project version changed. Refresh opportunities. Deleted evidence is never silently reused.

Why is “Extract with AI” disabled?

No API key is configured on this server. Use manual evidence or the local scanner. Once the operator configures a key and restarts, the app displays the connected model and daily request limit. Provider failures are visible; there is no fake successful fallback.

Why did my edit fail?

Another request changed the record, or the session expired. Keep a copy of unsaved text, refresh, then review the latest version. Conflicting edits do not silently overwrite newer work.

Can I invite teammates into my workspace?

Not in this version. Invitations create separate private accounts. Shared workspaces, automatic connectors, email delivery, subscription billing, and managed hosting remain separate deployment or future-product work.

I forgot my password.

Contact your server operator. The repository provides an authenticated filesystem recovery procedure. There is deliberately no unauthenticated account-reset web endpoint.
